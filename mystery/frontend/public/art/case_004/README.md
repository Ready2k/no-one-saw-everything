# Case 004 artwork

## Production Places scenes

The six `*_midnight_investigation_v2.png` files are the only approved
player-facing outdoor scenes for **The Fountain at Midnight**. They use the
cinematic 2.5D contract in `docs/scene_asset_bible.md`: 3840×2160, human
eye-level camera, realistic rural British materials, and foreground /
middle-ground / background separation.

The matching files under `intro/` ending in
`_midnight_investigation_v2_intro.jpg` are the approved Rewind derivatives.

The fountain and village-square images preserve the exact fountain, facades,
bench, lampposts, lanes, and paving established by
`/art/case_005/village_square_dawn_hd.avif` (also used by Case 010). Case 004
changes only the time, lighting, and camera distance. Their base frames
contain no body, blood, missing weapon stone, bootprint, lighter, culprit, or
police marker. Those details remain discovery-gated.

## Rejected legacy artwork

The earlier `*_moonlight_hd.png`, `fountain_closeup.avif`,
`fountain_daylight_map.png`, `fountain_midnight_map.avif`, and their older
intro derivatives are RPG/tile-art images. They are retained only as history:
do not wire them into the game, use them in the map editor, or cite them in
future prompts and manifests as style references.

The overhead Map Replay continues to use the canonical neutral village
defined in `docs/town_asset_bible.md`; Places artwork must not be substituted
for that map.
