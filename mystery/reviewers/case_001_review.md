# Case 001: The Storage Room Murder — Executive Showrunner Review
*Review pass 3 — post-fix. Supersedes pass 2 (13 Jul), which passed **Excellent** conditional on three P0 fixes. All three are now in the data; this pass re-scores the shipped case.*

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The flagship, and now without the asterisks. Pass 2 held this case at "Excellent, conditional"
on three defects: an objective deadline contradiction across four files, two innocence anchors
built on witnesses retracting their own statements, and Ben pre-solving the coat for the player.
All three are fixed in the current data. The deadline now reads "yesterday" everywhere
(`memories.json:36`, `clues.json:34/36`, `interviews.json:870`); Owen no longer claims he "was
alone the whole time"; Ben no longer says "that's Clara's coat, everyone knows it." What remains
is the case that was always underneath: a killer whose theft was an act of love, a weapon she
legitimately touches an hour before the murder, and a fifteen-minute window that turns the
village's geography into a trap.

The engine improvements since pass 2 also land here for free. Testimony-as-evidence means Clara's
fountain alibi can now be broken with Ben's sighting *as a witness statement*, not just as a
physical clue — the mechanic the game's title has always promised. The composure meter gives the
interrogation a live emotional read. Persistence means a player can leave and return mid-case.
None of these were authored for case_001 specifically; all of them make it better.

This is now a clean **Excellent**, and the benchmark the other five cases are measured against.

## INDIVIDUAL REVIEWER REPORTS

### Agent 1 — The Detective

**Logic Score:** 9/10 (restored from the pass-2 downgrade to 6)
**Fairness Score:** 9/10
**Difficulty Score:** 6/10

**Assessment:** The motive spine is now internally consistent. The ultimatum was issued
*yesterday* and the deadline was *this morning* — a coherent 24-hour clock that makes "why today?"
answerable. The weapon provenance remains the best fair-play seeding in the pack: Clara handles
the till weight at 07:36 in public, innocently, on the record, an hour before it kills Marcus. The
thud remains a single sound event that fixes time of death, method, and two witnesses'
whereabouts simultaneously.

**Remaining issues:**

1. **LOW CONFIDENCE — Difficulty still skews easy.** The chain from blue coat → damp cuff →
   Clara is short, and a careful player solves it inside twenty minutes. This is a deliberate
   design choice (the case's power is emotional, not combinatorial) and I no longer recommend
   hiding clues to fix it — pass 2's First-Time Player was right that the answer is *drama in the
   middle*, not *difficulty*. Noting it, not flagging it.
2. **LOW CONFIDENCE — The storage-room lock rota** (Clara's `location` answer plants a changed
   lock rota, then she enters through the rear door without comment) is still unpaid-off. It is a
   single clause and does not affect solvability. P2 at most.

**Suggested fixes:** None at P0/P1. The case is logically sound and fair.

### Agent 2 — The Screenwriter

**Entertainment Score:** 8/10
**Structure Score:** 8/10

**Tension curve:** Strong open (Owen's public row at 07:05). Genuine rise through the alley
sequence. The mid-game dip is *smaller* than at pass 2 — the Owen and Nadia anchors now read as
people withholding out of pride and confidentiality rather than clerical corrections, so the
elimination phase is about something. Sharp spike at Priya's confession. Quiet, non-triumphant
climax.

**Weakest Act:** Still Act 2, but it is no longer *administrative*. Owen would rather be suspected
than admit a delivery driver he owes money to is his alibi; Nadia withholds the blood-pressure
check because volunteering it means opening the door to patient confidentiality — the same door
she will later be asked to walk through for Clara. That is thematic rhyme the case now actually
collects.

**Strongest Act:** Act 3. Clara's `contradiction_locked` remains the best-written text in the
project: "He sat me down with his torn-out page like it was arithmetic. Like I was arithmetic."

**Highest ROI rewrite:** None urgent. If anything, a single wittier background beat to lift the
tone ceiling — but the melancholy is deliberate and the background NPCs already carry the charm.

**Damage report:** *Cleared.* The Owen retraction that pass 2 called "the one thing this character

### Agent 6 — Village Simulator

## SHOWRUNNER SUMMARY

Every reviewer confirms the three pass-2 defects are resolved and none introduced a regression. The

mechanic is independently praised by the Detective and the First-Time Player as making the middle

## TOP WEAKNESSES

1. **Ben and Priya's secret is disproportionate** — fear of a disapproving employer is not why
   people obstruct a murder inquiry. (Now the single largest remaining soft spot.)
2. **Difficulty is gentle** — deliberate, but it caps replay tension.
3. **The lock-rota line is planted and never paid off.**
4. **No humour in the principal cast** — deliberate, but it caps the ceiling.
5. **Ben remains thin** — one anecdote, one sighting, though he no longer over-reaches.
6. **Elias is a CCTV camera with opinions** — knowingly.
7. **Replay value is low** — once solved, solved; the epilogues are the reason to return.
8–10. No further material weaknesses at P0/P1.

## EXECUTIVE DECISION

### **Excellent.** Release.

**Would I fund it?** Yes, without hesitation. The flagship.

**Would I release it today?** **Yes.** The three pass-2 blockers are fixed and verified in data;
the case is logically sound, fair, and emotionally the strongest in the pack.

**Would I delay it?** No.

**Three highest-ROI improvements before launch:** All optional now — (1) raise the Ben/Priya stake;
(2) pay off the lock rota; (3) one wittier beat. None gate release.

- Stand alongside the best modern detective games? **Yes.**
- Remembered after one week? **Yes.** · One month? **Yes.** · One year? **Yes — the motive.**
- Recommend to friends? **Yes.** · Replay? **No, but they'll watch someone else play it.**
- Would streamers enjoy discovering it? **Yes.**

### "Why will players remember this mystery?"

Because it refuses them the pleasure of winning. Every mystery teaches the player to want the
killer caught; this one spends the hour teaching them to want *Clara* — competent, tired, fifteen
years behind that counter — and then makes them the reason she's arrested. The mop bucket is the
clue they'll describe to a friend; Fernlea is the thing they'll still be thinking about on the
drive home. Case 001 is the case in this pack that lands that gap, and it now lands it with none of
the pass-2 seams showing.
