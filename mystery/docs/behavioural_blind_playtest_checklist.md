# Behavioural System Blind Playtest Checklist

Use this with a new player who has not read the behavioural-tells spec. Do not explain Observe unless they get fully stuck.

## Setup

- [ ] Start on the intended active case and confirm the case label shown in the header, Suspects view, and Case Board matches.
- [ ] Ask the tester to think aloud while interviewing at least two suspects.

## Interview Discovery

- [ ] The tester notices the Observe control without a tutorial modal.
- [ ] The tester uses Observe after a calm answer, before pressure has risen.
- [ ] The calm read feels like a useful baseline, not a wasted action.
- [ ] After a later challenge or pressure rise, the tester notices a changed/deviation chip or stronger manner note.
- [ ] The tester describes the tell as something to interpret, not proof that the suspect is lying or guilty.

## Notebook

- [ ] The tester pins at least one observable tell or considered read.
- [ ] On the Case Board, the pinned item appears under the suspect's Behaviour subsection.
- [ ] The tester understands Behaviour is separate from hard evidence and contradiction notes.
- [ ] The grouping helps them compare manner changes without cluttering the evidence list.

## Layout

- [ ] On desktop, composure, portrait state, behavioural read, and considered read are readable at a glance.
- [ ] On a narrow/mobile viewport, Observe copy, comparison chips, and pinned-note titles wrap cleanly.
- [ ] With reduced motion enabled, read and portrait states remain understandable without animation.
